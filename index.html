Safe by design

Dares should be fun, not dangerous. Nothing is shown to anyone until it passes a safety check.

  • 🛡️ Every dare, photo and video is checked by AI before anyone sees it
  • 🧒 Under-18 accounts get only safe dares, start private, and get stricter checks
  • 🚩 Report, block and flag in two taps. Moderators review reports every day
  • 🙅 No sponsored dares. Ever.
  • 🔁 Unlimited retakes (3 free every day for everyone)
  • 🎬 20-second videos
  • 🛡️ A streak shield every month
  • 🎁 Gift DARE+ to a friend, and no ads

DARE+ is optional

Everything that matters is free. DARE+ adds extras, but it never helps you win: votes are votes.

DARE+ is an auto-renewing subscription, managed and cancelled in your App Store account settings.
