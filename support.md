# Support

Need help, found a bug, or want to report something? Email us at
**[support@getdareapp.com](mailto:support@getdareapp.com)** and we'll get back
to you, usually within 1–2 days.

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>When does the daily dare drop?</summary>
<p>Every day at a surprise moment between 12:00 and 21:00 (Türkiye time), the same for everyone. You'll get a notification, then you have 30 minutes. Late? You can still post until the day ends, but your post shows how many minutes late it was.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>Why can't I see my friends' posts?</summary>
<p>Friends' posts unlock after you post your own proof for today's dare. No peeking first 😉</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>Why is my post, photo or dare "being checked"?</summary>
<p>Everything goes through a quick safety check before anyone else can see it. It usually takes a few seconds. If something was held for a moderator, it can take longer.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>How many times can I dare a friend?</summary>
<p>You can dare the same friend once every 3 days, and send up to 2 friend dares per day, on top of the daily dare.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I report or block someone?</summary>
<p>Tap ••• on their post and choose Report, or open their profile and tap Report or Block. Our moderators review reports every day.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I cancel DARE+?</summary>
<p>DARE+ is managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → DARE, and tap Cancel. You keep DARE+ until the end of the period you paid for. For refunds, use <a href="https://reportaproblem.apple.com">reportaproblem.apple.com</a>.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I delete my account?</summary>
<p>In the app, go to Me → Settings → Delete account. This removes your profile, posts and photos. If you can't get into the app, email us from the address on your account and we'll do it for you.</p>
</details>

## Safety

If someone is in danger, contact your local emergency services first. To
report content that breaks the rules, use Report in the app or email
[support@getdareapp.com](mailto:support@getdareapp.com) with the username and
what happened.
