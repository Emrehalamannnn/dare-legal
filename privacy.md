# DARE Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 28, 2026_

DARE ("we", "us") is a social app where friends do a daily dare and vote on
each other's proof. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your
choices.

## Who we are

DARE is made by the DARE team.
Contact: **support@getdareapp.com**.

## What we collect

| What | Why | Where it's stored |
|---|---|---|
| Email address (from Sign in with Apple, Google or an email code) | To sign you in | Our database (Supabase) |
| Username, display name, avatar emoji and colour | Your profile | Our database |
| Birthdate | Only to keep under-18 players safe (safer dares, private by default, stricter checks). Never shown to anyone. You must be 13+. | Our database, in a private area the app can't read |
| Country and city you type in | City and country leaderboards | Our database |
| Profile photo and squad photos (optional) | Shown on your profile and your squads, after an automated safety check | Our storage (Supabase) |
| Photos, videos, reaction selfies and captions you post | To show your dare proof to friends, and to Discover if your account is public | Our storage (Supabase) |
| Dares you send, votes, friends, follows, streaks, trophies | To run the game | Our database |
| Purchases (DARE+, shields, shop items, gifts) | To give you what you bought | Our database; payment is handled by Apple, we never see your card |
| Which DARE+ screen you opened (for example from "See who voted") and whether a free trial or purchase followed | To see which parts of DARE+ people find useful. Our own database only: no outside analytics, never shared | Our database, deleted after 90 days; only anonymous totals are kept after you delete your account |
| Push notification token | To send the notifications you turned on | Our database |
| Crash reports from iOS (what the app was doing when it crashed, app and iOS version) | To fix bugs. Not linked to your account | Our database, deleted after 90 days |
| Your app's language | To send notifications and the daily dare in your language | Our database |
| Your phone's time zone (for example Europe/Berlin), its difference from UTC (for example +2 hours) and whether it shows 24-hour times | To put you in a Drop Zone (when the daily dare drops for you), to keep notifications out of the night, and to write times in notifications the way your phone does. Friends see your Drop Zone (for example "not dropped yet in the Americas"), never your time zone | Our database |
| When you last opened the app | To stop daily dare notifications when you haven't opened DARE for a few days (you then get at most 3 come-back messages) and to show "Welcome back" when you return. Never shown to anyone | Our database |
| Your "Suggest me to friends of friends" setting, and people you hid from People you may know | To suggest you (or not) to friends of your friends and people in your squads, and to not show you people you hid again | Our database |
| Squad invite codes (the code in a squad's invite link and QR code) | So people who get the link can join the squad. Its creator and admins can make a new one, and the old link stops working | Our database |

We don't use advertising trackers, we don't sell your data, and we don't
track you across other companies' apps or websites. The invite pages our
links open (getdareapp.com/add and /s) use no cookies and no tracking.

Some things stay on your phone and never reach us: what your Home Screen and
Lock Screen widgets show (your streak, today's dare, and which friends did it
with their names and emoji or profile photos; removed when you sign out),
your Sounds and Haptics choices, and the reminder before a DARE+ free trial
ends, which your phone schedules itself.

## Safety checks

To keep DARE safe, dares, captions, photos and still frames from videos are
checked by an automated safety service before others can see them. We use
**OpenAI's moderation service** for this; it receives the content only to
check it and doesn't use it to train models. Content involving under-18
players is checked more strictly. Reported content is checked again by the
same automated service, more strictly, and removed if it breaks the community
rules; accounts that break the rules can be suspended automatically. If you
think a decision was wrong, email us and a person will look at it.

## Who can see what

- **Friends** see your daily proof once their own proof passed the safety check, and your friend dares and duels.
- **Friends** also see which Drop Zone you're in (for example "Americas") while the dare hasn't dropped there yet.
- **Everyone** can see your daily posts on Discover only if your account is **public**. Under-18 accounts start private.
- **Profile photos:** an adult's profile photo is shown wherever their name is. An under-18's profile photo is only shown to their friends; everyone else sees their emoji avatar.
- **Hall of Fame** only shows you if you said yes.
- **Squads:** people in a squad see each other's names, avatars and weekly points on the squad's leaderboard, friends or not (a squad doesn't show them your posts; an under-18's photo only shows to their friends). Anyone with a squad's invite link can join; its creator and admins can reset the link and remove people, and anyone in a squad can report or block anyone in it. People who blocked each other don't see each other in a squad. An adult and an under-18 who share a squad but no friend see each other without the option to add each other as friends there, and the adult only sees the under-18's avatar and display name (no username or country). A squad's name is checked by the automated safety check before people outside the squad (for example someone opening its invite link) see it.
- **People you may know:** DARE may suggest you to friends of your friends and to people in your squads (with your name, username and avatar), so they can send you a friend request. It never uses anyone's contacts. An adult and an under-18 are only suggested to each other when they share at least 2 friends, or a squad and a friend, and an under-18 is never shown with their photo. It starts off for under-18s. You can turn it on or off in **Settings → Suggest me to friends of friends**.
- Blocked people can't see you.

## How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. When you delete your account
(**Settings → Delete account**), we delete your profile, posts, photos,
videos, votes, dares and everything else linked to it. Backups are removed
within **30 days**. The only thing kept is the record of which DARE+
screens were opened and whether a trial or purchase followed, with nothing
linking it to you any more: it counts only towards anonymous totals, and is
deleted after 90 days like everyone else's.

## Your rights

You can see and change your profile in the app, delete posts, make your
account private, turn notifications off, and delete your account at any
time. For anything else (for example a copy of your data), email **support@getdareapp.com**.
If you're in the EU/UK, you also have rights under the GDPR, including to
complain to your data protection authority.

## Children

DARE is for people **13 and older**. If you believe a child under 13 has
an account, email **support@getdareapp.com** and we'll delete it.

If someone gives a birthdate under 13 when signing up, no profile is made
and their sign-in account is deleted automatically within a day. So they
can't just try again with another birthdate, we keep only a scrambled
fingerprint (a salted hash) of the account's email, which can't be turned
back into the email, until the 13th birthday they gave. Then it's deleted too.

## Services we use

- **Supabase** (database, sign-in, storage) — servers in Frankfurt, Germany (EU)
- **Apple** (Sign in with Apple, In-App Purchases, push notifications)
- **OpenAI** (automated safety checks)
- **Google** (Google sign-in, if you choose it)
- **Resend** (sends the sign-in code emails)

## Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above and, for big changes,
tell you in the app.
