# DARE Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 29, 2026_

_Türkçesi (KVKK aydınlatma metni): https://getdareapp.com/tr/privacy_

DARE ("we", "us") is a social app where friends do a daily dare and vote on
each other's proof. This policy explains what we collect, why, who gets it,
how long we keep it, and your rights.

## Who is responsible

DARE is made by **Emre Halaman**, an independent developer in Türkiye, who is
responsible for your data (the "data controller" under the EU and UK GDPR and
Türkiye's KVKK).
Contact: **support@getdareapp.com**.

## Ads

If you don't have DARE+, the Friends feed may show a few ads from **Google
AdMob** between your friends' posts, once we turn ads on (they're off when
DARE launches). Never in the first 3 posts, never more than 3 at a time, and
never during your 30 minutes to post. DARE+ has no ads. Ads never change
votes, points, rankings or dares, and there are no sponsored dares.

To show and count these ads, Google's ad software in the app collects: your
device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking, see below), a
rough location from your IP address, which ads you see and tap, and technical
and diagnostic data about the app and your phone. Google uses this as
described in its policy at **https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads**.
We don't give Google your name, email, birthdate, posts or friends.

- **Personalized ads only if you allow them.** Ads are non-personalized
  unless you're 18 or older and say yes when your iPhone asks whether DARE may
  track you (Apple's "Allow tracking" question, asked right before your first
  ad in versions of DARE that ask it; the first version doesn't). If you say
  no, you still see ads, just less personal ones, and the advertising
  identifier isn't shared. You can change this any time in iPhone Settings →
  Privacy & Security → Tracking.
- **If you're in the EU, the UK or Switzerland**, Google's consent form asks
  you first what the ads may use (if you're 18 or older). You can change your
  choice any time in **Settings → Ad privacy choices**.
- **If you're in the United States**, personalized ads may count as "sharing"
  your data (for cross-context behavioral advertising) under some US state
  laws, such as California's. You can opt out any time in **Settings → Ad
  privacy choices** (in the states where it applies) or by choosing "Ask App
  Not to Track" when your iPhone asks (or later in iPhone Settings → Privacy &
  Security → Tracking); you then get only non-personalized ads.
- **If you're under 18**, you're never asked about tracking and never see
  Google's consent form: you only get non-personalized ads, marked for people
  under the age of consent, and only ads rated for teens (for general
  audiences if you're under 16). For this, the app gets only your age group
  from our server (never your birthdate), and Google gets no age at all, only
  the "under the age of consent" mark.

The ad software only starts for people who can get ads (not DARE+, and only
once we've turned ads on), and in the EU, the UK and Switzerland only after
Google's consent form.

Some things stay on your phone and never reach us: what your Home Screen and
Lock Screen widgets show (your streak, today's dare, and which friends did it
with their names and emoji or profile photos; removed when you sign out),
your Sounds and Haptics choices, and the reminder before a DARE+ free trial
ends, which your phone schedules itself.

## Safety checks

To keep DARE safe, dares, captions, names (display names, usernames and squad
names), photos and still frames from videos are checked by an automated
safety service before others can see them. We use **OpenAI's moderation
service** for this; it receives the content only to check it and doesn't use
it to train models. You agree to this when you first open DARE and when you
create your profile. Content involving under-18 players is checked more
strictly. Reported content is checked again by the same automated service,
more strictly, and removed if it breaks the community rules; accounts that
break the rules can be suspended automatically. These are automated
decisions: if you think one was wrong, email us and a person will look at it.

## Who can see what

- **Friends** see your daily proof once their own proof passed the safety check, and your friend dares and duels.
- **Friends** also see which Drop Zone you're in (for example "Americas") while the dare hasn't dropped there yet.
- **Everyone** can see your daily posts on Discover only if your account is **public**. Accounts of people 18 and older start public, under-18 accounts start private, and you can change yours any time in **Settings**.
- **Reaction selfies:** an adult's reaction selfie is seen by everyone who can see the post it's on (for a public post on Discover, that can be anyone; the app says so before you take it). An under-18 can only react to friends' posts, and only their own friends and the post's author see that reaction.
- **Profile photos:** an adult's profile photo is shown wherever their name is. An under-18's profile photo is only shown to their friends; everyone else sees their emoji avatar.
- **Hall of Fame** only shows you if you said yes.
- **Squads:** people in a squad see each other's names, avatars and weekly points on the squad's leaderboard, friends or not (a squad doesn't show them your posts; an under-18's photo only shows to their friends). Anyone with a squad's invite link can join; its creator and admins can reset the link and remove people, and anyone in a squad can report or block anyone in it. People who blocked each other don't see each other in a squad. An adult and an under-18 who share a squad but no friend see each other without the option to add each other as friends there, and the app shows the adult only the under-18's avatar and display name (no username or country). A squad's name is checked by the automated safety check before people outside the squad (for example someone opening its invite link) see it.
- **People you may know:** DARE may suggest you to friends of your friends and to people in your squads (with your name, username and avatar), so they can send you a friend request. It never uses anyone's contacts. An adult and an under-18 are only suggested to each other when they share at least 2 friends, or a squad and a friend, and an under-18 is never shown with their photo. It starts off for under-18s. You can turn it on or off in **Settings → Suggest me to friends of friends**.
- Blocked people can't see you.

Each of them gets only what its service needs and must protect it at least as
well as this policy does. Supabase, OpenAI and Resend work for us and use your
data only to provide their service to us; Apple and Google also follow their
own privacy policies for their sign-in, purchase, notification and ad
services.

## Sending data abroad

Our database is in the EU (Germany). OpenAI, Resend, Apple, Google and GitHub
may process data in the United States and other countries. When data leaves
the EU, the UK or Türkiye, we rely on the safeguards the law provides: the
EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the company is certified, or standard
contractual clauses (for Türkiye, the standard contracts under KVKK Article
9). Email us for a copy of the safeguards.

## How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. When you delete your account
(**Settings → Delete account**), we delete your profile, posts, photos,
videos, votes, dares and everything else linked to it. Backups are removed
within **30 days**. Apart from that, two things are kept:

- the record of which DARE+ screens were opened and whether a trial or
  purchase followed, with nothing linking it to you any more: it counts only
  towards anonymous totals, and is deleted after 90 days like everyone
  else's;
- if the safety check finds sexual content involving a minor, a copy of it
  and the account's id, in a locked area only the server can read, to report
  it to the authorities (for example NCMEC's CyberTipline). It's deleted one
  year after we reported it (the time US law asks us to keep it), or one year
  after it was found if it was never reported (for example because it was a
  mistake).

Crash and hang reports aren't linked to anyone and are deleted after 90 days.

## Your rights

You can see and change your profile in the app, delete posts, make your
account private, turn notifications off, turn off People you may know, and
delete your account at any time. You also have the right to:

- **access** your data and get a **copy** of it in a common format (portability);
- have it **corrected** or **deleted**;
- **restrict** or **object to** how we use it (for example our legitimate interests);
- **take back your consent** at any time (ads, tracking, notifications), without affecting what happened before;
- ask a person to look at an **automated decision** (for example when the safety check removed something or suspended you);
- if you're in Türkiye, every right in KVKK Article 11, including knowing who in Türkiye or abroad received your data and asking for compensation for unlawful processing.

Email **support@getdareapp.com** from the email address of your account and
we'll answer within one month (30 days under KVKK). You can also complain to
your data protection authority: in the EU your country's authority, in the UK
the ICO, and in Türkiye the KVKK Board (Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurulu), after
first writing to us.

## Children

DARE is for people **13 and older** (**16 and older in Australia and
Brazil**). If you believe a child under the minimum age has an account, email
**support@getdareapp.com** and we'll delete it.

If someone gives a birthdate under the minimum age when signing up, no profile
is made and their sign-in account is deleted automatically within a day. So
they can't just try again with another birthdate, we keep only a scrambled
fingerprint (a salted hash) of the account's email, which can't be turned
back into the email, until the birthday that would let them in (the 13th, or
the 16th in Australia and Brazil). Then it's deleted too.
