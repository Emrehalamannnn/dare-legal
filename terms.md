# DARE Terms of Use

_Last updated: September 25, 2026_

Welcome to DARE. By creating an account or using the app you agree to these
terms and to our [Privacy Policy]({{ "/privacy" | relative_url }}). If you don't agree, please don't
use DARE.

## 1. Who can use DARE

- You must be **at least 13 years old**.
- If you're under 18, you need a parent's or guardian's permission. Under-18
  accounts only get safe dares, start as private accounts, and every post is
  checked more strictly.
- You must give your real birthdate. We use it only to keep younger players
  safe, and nobody else can see it.
- One person, one account. Keep your sign-in private; you're responsible for
  what happens on your account.

## 2. Stay safe: dares are never worth getting hurt

DARE is for fun. **Never do anything dangerous, illegal, or that could hurt
you, someone else, or someone's property**, even if a dare seems to ask for
it. Skip it instead. You are always responsible for your own actions and
safety. Respect other people's privacy: don't film people who didn't agree to
it.

## 3. Community rules (zero tolerance)

You must not post, send or dare anything that:

- is dangerous, violent, or encourages self-harm, drugs, alcohol (for
  under-18s) or breaking the law;
- is sexual or nude, or sexualizes anyone, **especially anyone under 18**;
- bullies, harasses, threatens, or shows hate towards anyone;
- shares someone's private information (address, phone number and so on);
- is spam, a scam, advertising, or pretends to be someone else;
- breaks someone else's rights, like copyright.

**We have zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users.**
Content that breaks these rules is removed, and the account that posted it
can be suspended or banned without warning. Content involving the sexual
abuse of minors is reported to the authorities.

## 4. How we keep DARE safe

- Every dare, photo and video goes through an **automatic AI safety check**
  before anyone else can see it.
- You can **report** any post, dare or person, and **block** anyone, in two
  taps. Reports are handled **automatically within minutes**: reported content
  is checked again, more strictly, and removed if it breaks the rules, and
  accounts that break the rules are suspended.
- Think we got it wrong? Email us and a person will look at it.
- Automatic checks aren't perfect. If you see something that slipped through,
  please report it or email us.

## 5. Your content

You own the photos, videos and text you post. By posting, you give DARE a
free, worldwide license to store, show and share that content **inside DARE**
(for example to your friends, or on Discover if your account is public) so
the app can work. This license ends when you delete the content or your
account, except for copies we must keep for safety or legal reasons for a
short time. Only post content you have the right to post.

## 6. DARE+ and purchases

- **DARE+** is an optional auto-renewing subscription. Payment is charged to
  your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. It renews automatically

- Refunds are handled by Apple under its rules
  ([reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com)).
- DARE+ and shop items (like frames, effects and streak shields) are for fun.
  **They never help anyone win**: votes are votes. They have no cash value,
  can't be exchanged for money, and can't be moved to another account.
- Prices can change, but a change never affects a period you already paid for.

## 7. Ending your account

You can delete your account at any time in **Me → Settings → Delete
account**. This deletes your profile, posts, videos, votes and dares. We may
suspend or close accounts that break these terms.
